# Cyllex

Cyllex is a project that applies AI to real cybersecurity work: threat intelligence,
adversary emulation, detection, and sharing what we learn along the way.

The idea is to use AI to take the heavy part out of security research: reading piles of
reports, keeping track of what an adversary actually does, and turning that into something
you can work with. It's made by people who care about this and want to do it well.

## What we work on

- Threat intelligence: collecting sources, reading them, and building a clear, sourced
  picture of a threat.
- Adversary emulation: turning that intelligence into TTPs you can run.
- Detection: going from adversary behaviour to the rules that catch it.
- Knowledge: writing it down and sharing it openly.

## Projects

Argus. A local-first threat intelligence tool. It collects sources, reads them, and
consolidates them into a sourced, confidence-scored view of a threat, with a quote behind
every claim.
https://github.com/Cyllex-Framework/Cyllex-Argus

More tools for emulation and detection are on the way.
